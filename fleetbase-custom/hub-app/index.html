Gogate Products Hub

Sorting hubs for Fleetbase. Agents keep using Fleetbase Navigator.

Open Navigator

Hub sign in

Use the same Fleetbase account that signs in to the console. This does not create a second navigator.

Receive or dispatch

Scan the 12-digit AWB or the Fleetbase tracking number. The barcode has to match the shipment. Agents then open that job in Fleetbase Navigator.

Shipments

A new seller shipment stays on status Shipment Created until Navigator moves it. Assigning a driver sends the job to Fleetbase Navigator.

Sorting hubs

A hub is a sorting place. It is not the store. Enter the hub’s own name and address.

Drivers

Drivers sign in with Fleetbase Navigator. Block stops that login. Reset password here, then tell the driver the new password.
